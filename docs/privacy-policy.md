# Privacy Policy — Jordan XC Clinic app

**Last updated:** 18 September 2026
**Applies to:** the Jordan XC Clinic mobile app for iOS and Android
**Contact:** info@jordanxcclinic.com

> **Before this is published:** have whoever handles the clinic's business
> paperwork read this alongside the consent language in the app. It describes
> what the software actually does, which is the hard part — but it is not legal
> advice, and this app holds health information about children.

## Who we are

Jordan Cross Country Clinic ("the clinic", "we") runs youth cross country
coaching in Birmingham, Alabama. This app is the hub where athletes and their
parents or guardians find the schedule, their training, clinic news, and photos
after registering on jordanxcclinic.com.

Registration, payment, and liability waivers happen on the website, not in the
app. This policy covers the app only.

## What the app collects

**When you sign in.** The app uses Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google.
We receive your name and email address from whichever you choose. We never see
or store your Apple or Google password. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we
store the relay address Apple gives us and nothing else.

**Your clinic profile.** Your name, your role (athlete, parent, or coach), and
optionally a phone number.

**An athlete's intake form.** School, grade, personal best times, goals,
injury history, medical conditions, allergies and medication notes, and an
emergency contact's name, relationship, and phone number.

**Training activity.** Workouts an athlete marks as done, including distance,
time, how hard it felt, and any note written to the coach.

**Meetings.** Which one-on-one time you booked and what you said you wanted to
discuss.

**Photos.** Pictures coaching staff post from practices and meets.

## What the app does not collect

The app does not collect location, does not access your contacts or microphone,
does not use advertising identifiers, and contains no advertising or third-party
analytics. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell
personal information, and we never have.

## Why we hold it

- To show you the schedule, your training, and clinic news.
- So coaches know who is on the roster and who has been training.
- **So a coach at practice knows about the inhaler, the allergy, or the injury,
  and who to call.** This is why the health fields exist.

## Children's information

Most athletes in the clinic are under 18, and some are under 13.

- A child's intake form is visible only to that athlete, the parents or
  guardians linked to them, and clinic coaching staff. It is never shown to
  other families, and it does not appear in any list or search.
- **Every time a coach opens an athlete's intake form, we record who looked and
  when.** Parents may ask us for that record.
- We ask for a parent or guardian to confirm the health information may be held.
- We collect as little as the clinic can operate on. The app deliberately does
  not ask for a birth date, an address, or anything the registration on the
  website already holds.
- We do not knowingly let a child under 13 create an account without a parent
  or guardian involved. If you believe a child has an account you did not set
  up, email us and we will remove it.

## Who else sees it

**Supabase** hosts the database, the sign-in service, and the photo storage on
our behalf. They process the data under their terms and do not use it for their
own purposes.

**Apple** and **Google** handle sign-in. They tell us who you are; we do not
tell them anything about your training or health.

That is the complete list. No advertisers, no data brokers, no analytics
companies.

## How long we keep it

We keep an athlete's information for as long as they are part of the clinic.
Records for athletes who have been inactive for 24 months are deleted.

## Your choices

**See everything we hold.** Settings → Export my data hands you a complete copy.

**Delete your account.** Settings → Delete my account removes your profile,
intake form, personal bests, training logs, and bookings for good. It is
immediate and cannot be undone. A parent may do this on behalf of their child.

**Correct something.** Any athlete or their guardian can edit the intake form at
any time from the Profile tab.

**Ask us directly.** Email info@jordanxcclinic.com and we will help.

## Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is enforced in the
database itself, not only in the app, so an athlete's record cannot be reached
by another family even through a bug in the app. Photos are stored privately and
served through links that expire after an hour; there is no public address for
any photo.

## Changes

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and the date at the
top, and say so in the app.
