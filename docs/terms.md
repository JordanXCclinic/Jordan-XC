# Terms of Use — Jordan XC Clinic app

**Last updated:** 18 September 2026
**Contact:** info@jordanxcclinic.com

## What this app is

The Jordan XC Clinic app is an information hub for families already registered
with the clinic. It shows the practice schedule, training plans, clinic news,
educational sessions, photos, and lets families book a meeting with a coach.

Registration, payment, and liability waivers are handled on jordanxcclinic.com
and are not part of this app.

## Getting access

You need a clinic code, issued by the clinic after a family registers. Codes are
single use and tied to one athlete or one parent. Do not share your code — it
gives whoever holds it access to your athlete's information.

## Using it sensibly

- Keep the information in your athlete's profile accurate, particularly the
  emergency contact and anything medical. Coaches rely on it at practice.
- Do not share other families' information, including photos, outside the
  clinic.
- Do not attempt to reach records that are not yours.

We may remove access for anyone who misuses the app.

## Training is guidance, not medical advice

Workouts and educational sessions in this app are coaching guidance written for
the clinic's athletes. They are not medical advice. If an athlete is hurt or
unwell, see a doctor. Stop training and seek help rather than working through
pain.

## Photos

Photos are posted by coaching staff and are visible to clinic members only. If
you would rather your athlete did not appear in clinic photos, tell us and we
will remove them and keep them out.

## Your account

You may delete your account at any time from Settings. This permanently removes
your profile, intake form, personal bests, and training logs.

## Availability

We aim to keep the app running but cannot promise it is always available. The
schedule in the app is the clinic's best current information; weather and heat
change plans, and the announcement in the app is the authority.

## Changes

We will update this page and the date above if these terms change.
