## Description

Used for both stores. Play allows 4000 characters; this is well under.

```
The Jordan XC Clinic app is where athletes and their families find everything
about the summer cross country clinic in Birmingham, Alabama — the practice
schedule, their training, clinic news, and time with the coach.

This app is for families already registered with the clinic. You will need the
code you were given after registering at jordanxcclinic.com.

FOR ATHLETES
• Today's workout, written by your coach, with the distance and the effort
• Your whole training plan, week by week
• Mark a run as done, and tell your coach how it felt
• Practice times and meeting points, with changes flagged
• Coaching sessions on warming up, fuelling, and racing

FOR PARENTS
• The same app, pointed at your runner
• Log a run for an athlete who does not carry a phone
• Practice changes as they happen
• Your athlete's profile and emergency contact, kept up to date
• Book a one-on-one time with the coach

FOR COACHES
• Post practices, announcements and training plans
• See who has trained this week and how hard it felt
• Take the register at practice
• Issue the codes that let a family in

BUILT AROUND YOUNG ATHLETES
Most of our runners are under 18, so the app collects as little as possible.
It holds no health information at all — no injuries, conditions, allergies or
medication. Coach Will handles those conversations with parents directly.
A family's records are visible only to that athlete, their parents or
guardians, and clinic staff. There is no advertising, no tracking, and no
third-party analytics.

Registration, payment and waivers happen on jordanxcclinic.com. This app is
where the season lives afterwards.
```
